01 / Project Overview

From manual lead handling
to a connected workflow.

Lead enrichment and qualification involve more than collecting contact details. Businesses may need to check submitted information, gather company context, evaluate fit, and decide which leads deserve attention.

When these activities are handled separately, teams can spend time repeating tasks and transferring data between tools. A structured workflow can bring these steps together and apply consistent processing rules.

I built and tested this n8n workflow to practise designing connected automation steps, working with lead data, applying decision logic, and troubleshooting how information moves between nodes.

Business problem

  • Repetitive lead-processing tasks
  • Incomplete company and contact information
  • Time-consuming lead evaluation
  • Inconsistent handling of lead records
  • Manual handoffs between processing stages

03 / Process Breakdown

Breaking the workflow into stages.

The following stages describe the lead-management process this project is designed to support. The exact actions depend on the nodes and integrations configured in the workflow.

STEP 01

Lead Intake

Receive lead details as the starting point for subsequent processing.

STEP 02

Data Preparation

Map and prepare available fields so later workflow steps can use the required information.

STEP 03

Enrichment

Use the configured enrichment step to obtain additional context about a lead or its company.

STEP 04

Qualification

Apply defined business rules to evaluate lead attributes and determine the next processing path.

STEP 05

Conditional Routing

Direct records through different branches according to the conditions configured in n8n.

STEP 06

Data Handling

Pass the relevant lead information between connected steps while keeping the workflow organised.

STEP 07

Downstream Actions

Use the configured destination or notification steps to continue processing the resulting information.

STEP 08

Testing and Troubleshooting

Execute the workflow, inspect node outputs, and investigate integration or data issues during testing.

04 / Decision Logic

Using rules to guide decisions.

Qualification logic helps turn lead attributes into actionable outcomes. Rules should be explicit, testable, and aligned with the business process.

PATH 01

Priority Handling

Leads that meet the configured high-priority criteria can be directed to the relevant next step.

PATH 02

Further Review

Records that need more information or review can follow a separate processing path where configured.

PATH 03

Alternative Handling

Leads that do not meet a particular condition can be routed through the alternative branch.

The qualification rules and outcomes should be understood from the actual conditions configured in the workflow. These cards explain the purpose of branching, not fixed scoring thresholds.

06 / Business Value

Why this approach matters.

Reduced Repetition

Consistent Rules

Structured Data Flow

Practical Troubleshooting

07 / Key Learnings

Automation is more than connecting nodes.

Building and testing this project strengthened my understanding of how workflow structure, data mapping, conditional logic, and external integrations work together.

I also learned the importance of inspecting each node's output when something goes wrong. An issue that appears to be a workflow configuration problem may instead come from the data or the external service being used.

This project reflects my continued development in workflow automation and my interest in using practical automation to improve business processes.
