Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail sales dataset containing 10,025 transactions. The goal was to clean the raw data, explore sales patterns, investigate changes in performance, and build an interactive Excel dashboard for decision-making.

The analysis combines Excel data cleaning, PivotTables, Power Query, Power Pivot, and exploratory analysis to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

Exploratory Data Analysis

Key observation

Sales declined by 52.6% from August to September.

The decline coincided with a 54.4% reduction in transactions and a 49.9% reduction in unique customers, while average transaction value remained relatively stable.

Key Findings

01

September sales dropped sharply

Sales fell from approximately ₦76.78M in August to ₦36.40M in September.

02

Transaction volume also declined

Transactions fell from 1,068 in August to 487 in September.

03

Average transaction value remained stable

Average transaction value increased slightly from ₦71.89K in August to ₦74.75K in September.

04

Customer activity declined

Unique customers decreased from 865 in August to 433 in September.

05

Office Chair led revenue

Office Chair generated approximately ₦198.27M in total sales across the dataset.

06

Most sales had no discount

Transactions with a 0% discount generated approximately ₦388.95M in sales.
